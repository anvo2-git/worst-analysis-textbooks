The Worst Analysis Textbooks

A Design Exploration

25 iterations of landing pages for a site about the worst analysis textbooks ever written. A design exploration from chaos to polish — each version a different lens on the same absurd premise.

Phase 1: Go Wide

v1 – v12

Twelve wildly different directions. Brutalist neon, government hazard notices, conspiracy corkboards, horror posters — the goal was to explore every possible tone and see what sticks.

v1 Editorial / Academic Serif type, cream background, clean horizontal rules v2 Brutalist Neon green on black, Courier, thick red borders v3 1990s Retro / Y2K Comic Sans, starfield, marquee, under construction v4 Academic Journal Parody Scholarly layout, footnotes, two-column format v5 E-Commerce Parody Product cards, Prime badges, fake search bar v6 Dark Luxury / Gold Editorial Dark background, gold accents, prestige atmosphere v7 Magazine Retrospective Gradients, pull-quotes, multi-column editorial v8 Government Safety Notice Caution stripes, hazard reports, official seals v9 Review Aggregator / Yelp Parody Red header, sidebar filters, star ratings v10 Yahoo 360 Blog Parody Mid-2000s purple/blue, profile sidebar, mood tracker v11 Conspiracy Corkboard Pushpins, red string, classified documents v12 Horror Movie Poster Dark cinematic style, taglines, content warnings

Phase 2: Combine

v13 – v19

Taking the strongest ideas from Phase 1 and mashing them together. What happens when you cross a conspiracy board with luxury editorial? Time to find out.

v13 Authentic Yahoo 360 Faithful recreation with tab nav, Blast, friends grid v14 MathAdvisor Gold Review aggregator layout with dark luxury gold palette v15 Dark Luxury Blog Dark editorial with long-form essay reviews v16 Social Profile Goodreads-style profile with review cards and shelves v17 Minimal Modern Stripped-down warm grid, gold accents, generous whitespace v18 Dark Mode MathAdvisor Full review site in dark theme with gold accents v19 Light Academic Blog Warm Substack-style blog, serif type, burgundy accent

Phase 3: Polish

v20 – v25

The final stretch. v19's light academic blog won the vote. Six rounds of refinement — typography, content, visuals, layout, responsiveness — converging on the definitive version.

v20 Typography Refinement Better sizing hierarchy, vertical rhythm, tag styling v21 Curated Reviews Best reviews from the content bank, including Today You Tomorrow Me v22 Visual Polish Paper texture, star ratings, pull quotes, floating paper shadow v23 Layout Enhancements Drop caps, post numbers, reading progress, subscribe box v24 Responsive + Micro-interactions Fluid type, hover states, print styles, accessibility v25 The Epsilon Errata — Final Everything combined. Six reviews. The definitive version.
